# References

Every figure Kuhira uses, and where it comes from. Page numbers are the printed page numbers.

## Data

1. **National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR).** *Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS), 2020–2025: public-use microdata,
   Seasons A and B.* NISR Microdata Catalog, <https://microdata.statistics.gov.rw/>. Accessed October 2026 under NISR's
   public-use data terms; the microdata is not redistributed in this repository.
2. **NISR.** *Seventh Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey (EICV7) 2023/24: Main Indicators Report*, Table
   A9.23 (district poverty rates). July 2025.
   <https://www.statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-07/EICV7_Main%20Indicator%20Report.pdf>
3. **Funk, C., Peterson, P., Landsfeld, M., et al. (2015).** The climate hazards infrared precipitation with stations — a
   new environmental record for monitoring extremes. *Scientific Data* 2, 150066. CHIRPS v2.0 monthly Africa grids,
   January 1995 to August 2026, from the Climate Hazards Center, UC Santa Barbara, <https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps>.
4. **Runfola, D., Anderson, A., Baier, H., et al. (2020).** geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative
   boundaries. *PLoS ONE* 15(4): e0231866. Rwanda ADM2 (districts), CC BY 4.0, <https://www.geoboundaries.org/>.

## Policy figures

5. **Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources (MINAGRI).** *Strategic Plan for Agriculture Transformation 5 (PSTA5),
   2024/25–2028/29.* Small-scale irrigation (SSIT) target of 41,031 ha by 2028/29.
6. **MINAGRI.** *Annual Report FY 2024/2025.* SSIT area of 27,663 ha (p. 6); 1,462 ha of new irrigation in FY2024/25
   (p. 19); NST2 and PSTA5 progress (p. 11) and Annex 8, "Progress toward PSTA5 and NST2" (p. 107): area under
   irrigation 74,375 ha against a 76,686 ha target, bean yield 0.7 against a 0.8 t/ha target.
7. **World Bank (2023).** *Project Appraisal Document: Second Sustainable Agricultural Intensification and Food Security
   Project (SAIP II, P181077)*, Report No. PAD5488, 3 October 2023, pp. 19–20. SSIT matching grant: 50% of regular
   technology, up to 75% for solar pumps.
8. **Akaliza, N., Bodnar, L., Brozović, N., Mukarusagara, G., Nzayiramya, S., Turatsinze, F., Urujeni, R. (2022).**
   *Mapping and analysis of the business ecosystem for smallholder irrigation in Rwanda.* Daugherty Water for Food
   Global Institute, University of Nebraska, April 2022, p. 16. Kit prices: treadle pump RWF 90,000; petrol pumps
   RWF 87,500–300,000; pipes RWF 83,500–150,000.
9. **Daugherty Water for Food Global Institute (2019).** "How a farmer coop may be key to unlocking agricultural potential
   in Rwanda", 11 November 2019 (citing RAB: equipment costs around $1,500 per hectare).
   <https://waterforfood.nebraska.edu/news-and-events/news/2019/11/how-a-farmer-coop-may-be-key-to-unlocking-agricultural-potential-in-rwanda>
10. **The New Times (2015).** "Farmers to benefit from enhanced irrigation", 26 July 2015 (MINAGRI: small-scale
    irrigation costs about $1,500 per hectare).
    <https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/121034/News/farmers-to-benefit-from-enhanced-irrigation/amp>
